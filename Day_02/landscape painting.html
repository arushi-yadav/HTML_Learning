1.Light does the talking; the brush just listens.


2.Every horizon holds a small forgiveness.


3.Stillness has a color, and it lives in the fields at dusk.


4.Paint the wind, not just the trees it moves.


5.Somewhere between the hills and the haze, the truth of a place appears.


6.Distance is a color too, if you look softly enough.
